# YouTube packaging

A good video with a weak package is invisible. Package after the cut, using frames that already
exist; do not promise something the film does not deliver.

## Steps

1. **Title.** One concrete promise, at most 60 characters. Name the idea, not the tool. Avoid
   “Introducing”, “unlock”, “seamless” and “in seconds” unless measured.
2. **Thumbnail bets.** Render three variants from the strongest stills: a face-free diagram, a
   number/claim, and a before/after. Check each at 320×180. No tiny text, no three-card rows.
3. **Description.** First two lines state the takeaway and who it is for. Then a short summary,
   chapter list with timestamps, sources and credits.
4. **Chapters.** 4–8 chapters, each a question or a turn; timestamps match the manifest, not a guess.
5. **Private check.** Upload a private draft, watch the first 30 s and the last 30 s, check A/V drift,
   captions, loudness and the end card. Only then schedule.
6. **Evidence.** Keep the title, three thumbnails, description and chapter timestamps in
   `docs/packaging/` or the PR body.

## Completion criteria

- The title is legible at a glance and not clickbait.
- Three thumbnails exist and are judged at phone size.
- The description is accurate and the chapters match the master.
- The private upload plays with captions and audio.
- A human has approved the package.
